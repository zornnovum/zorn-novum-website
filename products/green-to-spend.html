Know what’s left after the bills.

A paycheck can make your balance look comfortable—until you remember what still has to come out. Green to Spend helps you account for income, bills and reserves, so you can see what your plan leaves available for everyday spending.

A clearer view between paydays

Make the next spending decision with the bills in view.

Green to Spend starts with the money you expect and the obligations you need to cover. Add your accounts, income, bills and reserves; the app uses those entries to show a Green to Spend amount for your plan.

Keep the picture current as life happens

Everyday actions are designed to be straightforward: record when you got paid, made money, paid a bill or spent money. As you update the plan, the available amount can reflect those changes.

Green to Spend is built around manual entries in the current test experience. It helps organize the information you enter; it does not replace checking your accounts or deciding what is right for you.

Green to Spend FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does “Green to Spend” mean?

It’s the amount the app calculates as available in your plan after accounting for the income, bills and reserves you’ve entered. It’s meant to make your day-to-day budget easier to understand.

Does it connect to my bank?

Not in the current test build. The experience is based on information you enter manually. Bank connectivity is planned for a future Premium Plus offering, but it is not available yet.

Are backups available?

Online backups are planned for a future Premium Plus offering. Backups are not enabled in the current test environment.

When will Green to Spend launch?

There is no committed public launch date. We’re refining the first-time setup and core experience before making release plans.

What will it cost?

Subscription options and prices are planned, but Google Play purchasing has not been fully configured and tested. We’ll confirm what is available before release.

What happens after I sign up?

You’ll receive occasional Green to Spend development and launch updates. We won’t send a public launch announcement until availability is confirmed, and you can unsubscribe at any time.
