Help getting your app ready for customers

Built an app with an AI app builder? Let’s work out what it needs next.

You’ve put time, money and thought into an app you’re proud of. If getting it ready for customers has become more complicated than you expected, we can help you understand what you have and plan the next step.

Build on what you’ve started

Practical help, with a person working alongside you.

You don’t need to know all the technical terms to start a conversation. Tell us what your app does, what you like about it and where you’re stuck.

Understand what’s behind your app

We can review the available code and the services your app relies on, including where its data lives, how people sign in and how it connects to other tools.

Fix and improve what you’ve built

We can investigate problems, refine features and identify work needed for the customer experience you have in mind, including payments and access where relevant.

Explore more control

If you want to move beyond your original builder, we can assess what can be carried over, what needs adapting or replacing, and suitable hosting and handover options.

How we work together

Start with your app and your goals.

  1. Talk through what you have

    Describe the builder you used, what already works and what you want to do next. A short description is enough for the initial conversation.

  2. Understand the options

    We assess the available code, services and access, then explain practical options and the work they may involve. A deeper technical review may need a separately agreed scope.

  3. Agree the work and handover

    Before paid work begins, we agree the deliverables, price, review steps and responsibilities, including source-code handoff and guidance for operating the app where included.

A realistic path forward

More control starts with understanding the dependencies.

An exported app may still rely on the original platform for its database, sign-in, file storage or server-side features. Moving it can involve adapting code, moving data, replacing services or rebuilding parts of the app.

What’s possible depends on the app, available exports, access and platform terms. We explain those limits before agreeing a transition. Supported devices, distribution, ongoing costs and optional hosting are agreed for your project; app-store approval is not guaranteed.

The initial consultation and project quote are free. Technical review, development and migration are individually scoped and quoted.

Before you get in touch

A few useful answers.

Do I need to know how to code?

No. Explain what you want the app to do and where you need help. We’ll explain the technical choices in plain language.

Will the whole app need to be rebuilt?

Not necessarily. We first assess what can be reused and what depends on the original builder. The answer varies by app.

Can I run it using my own accounts?

Where suitable, we can assess deployment into accounts you control, source-code handoff and practical operating guidance. Third-party services and ongoing costs may still be needed. The agreement specifies the files, rights, setup and support included.
